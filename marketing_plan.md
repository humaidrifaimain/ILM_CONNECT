# IlmBit - Marketing & Launch Plan

## 1. Target Audience
* **Primary:** Muslim parents in the diaspora (UK, Europe, Australia, North America, Middle East) seeking reliable, high-quality Quran and Islamic education for their children.
* **Secondary:** Adult diaspora Muslims looking to study advanced topics like Hadith, Fiqh, and the Arabic language on a 1-on-1 basis.
* **Supply Side:** Qualified Sri Lankan Islamic scholars (Maulavis) looking for a reliable, global teaching platform to generate a sustainable income.

## 2. Value Proposition (The "Pitch")
* **For Students/Parents:** "Authentic, structured 1-on-1 Islamic education from qualified scholars, delivered seamlessly to your home." No more struggling with unreliable scheduling or disorganized payments.
* **For Scholars:** "Focus on teaching; we handle the rest." A dedicated platform that handles global payments, student acquisition, and Zoom scheduling automatically.

## 3. Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-4)
* **Social Media Setup:** Create professional profiles on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. 
* **Content Strategy:** 
  * Share short clips of scholars explaining basic Islamic concepts (builds trust).
  * Post infographics on the importance of structured learning (e.g., the 3-day gap rule).
  * Highlight the seamless tech experience (Dashboard previews, auto-recorded sessions).
* **Waitlist Campaign:** Drive traffic to a simple landing page offering a "Free 30-Minute Trial Session" for the first 100 signups. 

## 4. Launch Phase (Weeks 5-8)
* **Influencer Partnerships:** Partner with micro-influencers in the Muslim diaspora communities (UK, Australia, Canada). Offer them a free month of the Premium Tier to review the platform on Instagram/TikTok.
* **Paid Ads (Meta & Google):** 
  * *Facebook/Instagram Ads:* Target diaspora parents with messaging around "Finding a reliable Quran teacher for your kids just got easier."
  * *Google Search Ads:* Target high-intent keywords like "Online Quran teacher UK", "Learn Fiqh online 1-on-1", "Islamic classes for kids online".
* **Scholars as Advocates:** Encourage onboarded Sri Lankan scholars to share their new teaching profiles on their own networks to kickstart the marketplace.

## 5. Post-Launch & Growth (Weeks 9+)
* **Referral Program:** Implement a "Refer a Family" program where existing students get a discount on their next month's subscription if they bring in a new student.
* **SEO & Content Marketing:** Start publishing blog posts on the IlmBit website (e.g., "How to keep your child engaged in online Quran classes", "Understanding the basics of Fiqh"). 
* **Community Engagement:** Sponsor local diaspora community events, Islamic center newsletters, or online webinars featuring IlmBit scholars.

## 6. Key Metrics to Track
* **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** How much we spend on ads to get one paying student.
* **Conversion Rate:** Landing page visitors → Free Trial → Paid Subscription (Tiers 1, 2, or 3).
* **Churn Rate:** Ensure students are staying month-over-month. Highlight the monthly progress reports (Tier 2) as a retention tool.
